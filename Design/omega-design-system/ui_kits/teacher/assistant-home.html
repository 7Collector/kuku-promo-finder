Tomorrow's lesson plan

Solving equations with the variable on both sides

Filled in from the next topic in your syllabus. Change it if tomorrow is different.

Objectives

Move the variable terms to one side by doing the same thing to both sides.
Check a solution by substituting it back into the equation.

Lesson sequence

5 min

Recap: equations with the variable on one side.

Teacher

Put 3x + 5 = 20 on the board and ask for the first step.

Students

Solve it in their notebooks and compare with a partner.

20 min

Worked examples with the variable on both sides.

Teacher

Solve 5x − 3 = 2x + 9, naming each balancing step.

Students

Predict the next step before it is written.

Total 45 minutes

Board work
