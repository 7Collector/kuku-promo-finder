## Problem 1: Find the Promos (Ads) ← **CHOSEN**

> Build a tool that reads a microdrama screenplay and finds the best promos in it.

### The Situation

Today an editor watches every episode and picks promo moments by hand, one show at a time, and we release new shows every week.

### What We Mean by a Promo

A promo is a **20 to 60-second clip** taken from the show itself and posted on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube to pull new viewers in. It's usually one continuous stretch, but it can also join a few moments, from one episode or across several. A good promo:

1. **Opens on a hook** — something in the first 3 seconds that makes a scrolling viewer stop (a slap, a reveal, a shocking line)
2. **Ends on a cliffhanger** — cuts at an unresolved moment so the viewer needs to know what happens next
3. **Makes sense on its own** — to someone who has never seen the show

### Your Task

1. **Find the promos** across all episodes. For each one, give the episode and the start/end timestamps of every segment, in order, plus the hook, the cliffhanger, and one line on why it works.
2. **Rank them.** Which promos would you post first, and why?
3. **Check quality.** Where does your tool pick badly?
